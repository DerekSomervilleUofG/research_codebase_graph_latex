\subsection{Normality and homoscedasticity - For all components touched for all commits} 

Certain statistical methods require normality of each sample or the equality of variance. A Shapiro-Wilk Test evaluates whether a sample distribution significantly differs from the normal distribution. A Levene's test evaluates if the independent samples have equal variance.  

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\caption{Diagnostic $P$-Value Thresholds Required for Statistical Method Selection}

\midrule
\textbf{Student's $t$-test} & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & $\ge 0.05$ (Equal Variance) \\
\textbf{Standard ANOVA}     & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & $\ge 0.05$ (Equal Variance) \\
\textbf{Welch's $t$-test}   & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & $\ge 0.05$ (Normal) & \textless{} 0.05 (Unequal Variance) \\
