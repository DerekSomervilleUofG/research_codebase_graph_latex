\section{Glossary - Summary} 
\begin{itemize} 
 \item A founder developer starts in the first three months of a project. 
\item  A Later joiner developers start after six months. 
\item Transient developers have nine (9) or less commits. 
\item Moderate developers have more than nine (9) commits but fewer than 50 commits or commits for less than 250 days. 
\item Sustained developers make 50 or more commits and commit for 250 days or more. 
\end{itemize}
